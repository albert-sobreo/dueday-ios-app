DueDay

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The short version: DueDay does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

DueDay (“the app”) is a bill tracker made by Jan Albert Sobreo. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores

The bills, accounts, profiles, amounts, due dates, payment records, logos and settings you enter are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them, and they are never sent to a server.

Camera, photos and files

If you scan a paper bill, or import a photo, screenshot or PDF, the app reads the text on your device to fill in the amount, due date and account number. The image is not uploaded anywhere. If you choose to attach a scan to a bill, it is saved on your device with that bill. Logos you upload are also stored only on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with DueDay, your requests are handled by Apple on your device under Apple’s own privacy practices.

Tips

DueDay is free. You can leave an optional tip. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details or personal information from you.

Third parties

DueDay contains no third-party code that collects data. It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Backups and deleting your data

Your device’s own backup (for example, iCloud Backup) may include DueDay’s data, under Apple’s terms. To delete all your DueDay data, delete the app from your iPhone.

Children

DueDay is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if sync is added in a future version, this page and the date above will be updated.
