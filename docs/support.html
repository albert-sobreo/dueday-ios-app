Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. DueDay has no accounts. Your bills are stored on your iPhone.

I didn’t get a reminder.

Open Settings in DueDay and check that notifications are on. Also check iOS Settings › Notifications › DueDay, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing it. Reminder time can be changed in DueDay › Settings › Reminders.

What is a cutoff?

A cutoff is the stretch of the month between two paydays. Set your paydays in Settings › Cutoffs and DueDay adds up the bills in each one, so you know how much to set aside before you’re paid.

Why does a variable bill (like electricity) not repeat?

Amounts change each month, so DueDay saves the account and you add each month’s bill when it arrives. Scan the paper bill or type it in, and the account details are already filled in.

Is my scanned bill uploaded anywhere?

No. Scans are read on your device and never leave it.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

DueDay data is included in your iPhone backup. Restoring a backup to the new phone brings your bills with it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. All of its data is removed with it.
